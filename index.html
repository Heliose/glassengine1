HOW IT WORKS

A jet engine and an electric drive unit cut from crystal. Light splits into colour at every edge, focuses into caustics on the floor, and an inner fire lights the hot section like jewellery. Hold still and the picture refines itself, frame by frame, into a photoreal still.

    Everything is procedural — no model files, no textures. Built on three.js r186.
